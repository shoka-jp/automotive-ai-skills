# Vehicle service and sales verification sources / 整備・販売の確認資料

Use these primary sources when a workflow needs current recall, estimate, or advertising information. Reopen the source for the relevant market at the time of use; this page is a starting point, not a statement that every vehicle or seller is covered. Links checked: 2026-10-29.

リコール、見積もり、広告表示を確認するための一次資料です。利用時に対象市場の最新情報と適用範囲を確認してください。特定車両や販売者への適用を保証する一覧ではありません。リンク確認日: 2026-10-29。

| Source / 情報源 | Use / 用途 | Boundary / 確認上の注意 |
|---|---|---|
| [NHTSA recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) | US vehicle-specific and general campaign searches / 米国の個別車両・届出検索 | VIN lookup does not show every past or recently announced campaign, completed repair, or foreign-market vehicle. Read the current coverage notes. / 全履歴・直近情報・改修済み・他市場の車両を網羅するとは限りません。 |
| [MLIT vehicle applicability FAQ](https://www.mlit.go.jp/jidosha/carinf/rcl/faq_sub/answer007.html) | Japan: why a published chassis range needs manufacturer confirmation / 日本: 車台番号の範囲と個別適用の違い | A vehicle in the published range may still be outside the campaign; confirm with the manufacturer. / 範囲内でも対象外の場合があり、メーカーで確認します。 |
| [MLIT recall search](https://renrakuda.mlit.go.jp/renrakuda/recall-search.html) | Find Japanese campaign notices by vehicle/type/date / 車名・型式・届出日による検索 | A notice does not prove repair completion for a particular vehicle. / 届出は個別車両の改修完了の証明ではありません。 |
| [FTC Auto Repair Basics](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/0211-auto-repair-basics) | US consumer guidance on written estimates, questions, and repair records / 米国の見積もり・確認事項・修理記録の案内 | Local requirements and the actual contract still need checking. Do not apply US guidance as another country's law. / 地域の要件と契約を別途確認し、他国の法律として扱いません。 |
| [AFTC used-car advertising guidance](https://www.aftc.or.jp/contents/am/kiyaku/chuuko/hitsuyou_1.html) | Japan: vehicle, condition, warranty, and price disclosures / 日本: 車両・状態・保証・価格の表示 | Confirm seller/platform applicability and current definitions, including 修復歴. / 売主・媒体への適用と修復歴等の定義を確認します。 |
| [AFTC total-price guidance](https://www.aftc.or.jp/contents/am/shiharai/index.html) | Japan: total price and its breakdown / 日本: 支払総額と内訳 | Verify the transaction's conditions and which costs belong in each component; do not invent fees or tax rates. / 販売条件と費用区分を確認し、費用や税率を作りません。 |

## Evidence record / 根拠の記録

For each material claim, record the source URL or document, check date, market, vehicle match evidence (redacted where appropriate), the actual finding, and what is still unknown. Source access failure is an unverified check, not a negative result. Full VINs, chassis numbers, owner addresses, and payment details do not belong in public reports unless a specific required disclosure justifies the relevant field.

重要な主張ごとに、URL・書類、確認日、市場、必要に応じて伏せた個別照合の根拠、実際の結果、未確認事項を残します。アクセスできない場合は「未確認」であり「該当なし」ではありません。公開資料では必要な表示を守りつつ、VIN・車台番号の全体、所有者住所、決済情報などを不要に掲載しません。
