# Honda N-BOX JF3/JF4 — low-pressure fuel pump recall

**English** | [日本語](./honda-nbox-jf3-jf4-fuel-pump.ja.md)

## Scope

- **Vehicles:** N-BOX and N-BOX Custom, DBA-JF3/JF4 and 6BA-JF3/JF4 rows in recall 5423.
- **Engines/components:** Low-pressure fuel pump and its resin impeller.
- **Production window:** Listed subranges span 2017-07-26 to 2020-02-14 for JF3 and to 2020-02-12 for JF4. They are not continuous eligibility ranges; use the complete manufacturer table.
- **Markets:** Japan. The same notice lists other Honda models; their ranges are not interchangeable with N-BOX ranges.

## The issue

The impeller can deform and prevent the pump from operating, potentially stalling the engine. The 2024-06-14 correction requires checking the pump identification mark and replacing the pump when applicable; a vehicle inside a listed range is not automatically a replacement case.

## Symptoms an owner can detect

- Difficult starting, failure to accelerate, vibration, or stalling, as described in the Honda notice.
- Record the conditions and existing warning messages; do not keep driving to reproduce a suspected fuel-supply failure.

## Severity

**Critical.** Loss of engine power while driving is a safety concern.

## Frequency (honest assessment)

The cited manufacturer information does not establish a failure probability for this vehicle or a mileage-based failure rate. A campaign population is not a count of failed vehicles; do not describe every example as defective.

## Fix and cost

- **Repair:** Have Honda check applicability, the pump identification, and the latest remedy/completion record. Fuel-system access and replacement belong to qualified technicians; this entry supplies no tank-removal or pump-opening procedure.
- **Typical cost:** Japan: confirm individual eligibility and the current remedy terms with the dealer first. No verified general retail price is supplied. Do not promise that all associated repairs are free; obtain an itemized quote for work outside the remedy.

## Buyer checklist

- Distinguish “not subject after identification check,” “replacement completed,” and “not yet verified.”
- Confirm the date and scope of the dealer result; earlier fuel-pump campaigns do not establish the current status.
- Do not quote an old parts-supply announcement as a current appointment or completion promise.

Example prompt for an evidence-based check:

```text
2019 Japan-market N-BOX JF3: the chassis appears in a published range, but the seller says the pump needs no replacement. What identification and completion evidence should I request?
```
