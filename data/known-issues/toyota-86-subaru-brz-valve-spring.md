# Toyota 86 / Subaru BRZ — valve spring recall

**English** | [日本語](./toyota-86-subaru-brz-valve-spring.ja.md)

## Scope

- **Vehicles:** Toyota 86 DBA-ZN6 and Subaru BRZ DBA-ZC6; this entry does not cover the other Subaru models in the same notice.
- **Engines/components:** Valve springs in the engines fitted to the listed ZN6/ZC6 vehicles.
- **Production window:** 86: 2012-03-12 to 2013-07-02; BRZ: 2012-03-09 to 2013-07-02. These are production windows, not proof that every vehicle in the interval is affected.
- **Markets:** Japan. Confirm a Japanese-market import with its original-market manufacturer; do not reuse these ranges as overseas campaign coverage.

## The issue

Recall 4361, filed on 2018-11-01, concerns springs that can fracture. Engine noise or poor running can progress to a stall. Identify applicability and repair completion separately; a model-year match is insufficient.

## Symptoms an owner can detect

- New engine noise, rough running, or stalling; these symptoms also have other causes.
- A quiet engine does not establish campaign completion. Ask the dealer about any existing symptom before arranging a viewing or test drive.

## Severity

**Critical.** A stall while driving can create a safety hazard.

## Frequency (honest assessment)

The cited manufacturer information does not establish a failure probability for this vehicle or a mileage-based failure rate. A campaign population is not a count of failed vehicles; do not describe every example as defective.

## Fix and cost

- **Repair:** The published remedy is replacement with revised springs. Have the authorized workshop confirm the applicable current procedure and record the campaign completion; do not infer completion from an unrelated engine repair invoice.
- **Typical cost:** Japan: confirm individual eligibility and the current remedy terms with the dealer first. No verified general retail price is supplied. Do not promise that all associated repairs are free; obtain an itemized quote for work outside the remedy.

## Buyer checklist

- Compare the chassis identifier privately against the Toyota or Subaru notice and official lookup.
- Obtain the specific campaign number, completion date, and workshop record, rather than a general “recalls done” statement.
- Keep current symptoms on a separate diagnostic list; recall completion does not certify overall engine condition.

Example prompt for an evidence-based check:

```text
2013 Japan-market Toyota 86 ZN6: the seller has an engine repair invoice but no recall completion result. Separate what is known from what I should ask about campaign 4361.
```
