# Daihatsu Tanto LA600S/LA610S — brake booster corrosion

**English** | [日本語](./daihatsu-tanto-la600s-brake-booster-corrosion.ja.md)

## Scope

- **Vehicles:** Tanto DBA-LA600S/LA610S in the 2019-04-11 notice. Other Daihatsu models have their own ranges.
- **Engines/components:** Water sealing at the cowl/dash-panel joint and the brake booster.
- **Production window:** LA600S: 2013-09-26 to 2016-10-04; LA610S: 2013-09-27 to 2016-10-04. Check the actual chassis range and status.
- **Markets:** Japan; do not generalize the notice to later Tanto generations.

## The issue

Water reaching the booster can cause corrosion and, if it progresses to perforation, reduced brake assistance. Recall 4471 addresses both the water-sealing cause and the condition of the booster.

## Symptoms an owner can detect

- A brake pedal requiring unusually high effort or a change in braking performance needs prompt professional assessment.
- Do not arrange repeated braking tests to confirm the defect. Visible appearance alone cannot establish booster condition or recall completion.

## Severity

**Critical.** Reduced brake assistance can increase the distance needed to stop.

## Frequency (honest assessment)

The cited manufacturer information does not establish a failure probability for this vehicle or a mileage-based failure rate. A campaign population is not a count of failed vehicles; do not describe every example as defective.

## Fix and cost

- **Repair:** The notice calls for a revised water seal and booster inspection, followed by replacement or corrosion protection according to its condition. Have qualified brake-system technicians perform the work; a seal-only receipt does not establish that all required actions were completed.
- **Typical cost:** Japan: confirm individual eligibility and the current remedy terms with the dealer first. No verified general retail price is supplied. Do not promise that all associated repairs are free; obtain an itemized quote for work outside the remedy.

## Buyer checklist

- Ask for the full campaign completion record and the booster inspection result.
- If brake feel is abnormal, have the workshop decide safe transport before any purchase test drive.
- Separate cosmetic rust elsewhere on the car from this specific brake-system assessment.

Example prompt for an evidence-based check:

```text
2014 Japan-market Tanto LA600S: a seller has a water-seal repair receipt but no booster inspection result. What should be verified for recall 4471 before a test drive?
```
