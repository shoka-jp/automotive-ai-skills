# Honda Fit GK3–GK6 / GP5–GP6 — low-pressure fuel pump recall

**English** | [日本語](./honda-fit-gk-gp-fuel-pump.ja.md)

## Scope

- **Vehicles:** Fit DBA-GK3/GK4/GK5/GK6 and DAA-GP5/GP6 rows in recall 5423, including the hybrid variants.
- **Engines/components:** Low-pressure fuel pump impeller; not the i-DCD transmission.
- **Production window:** The Fit rows contain separate subranges within 2017-06-20 to 2019-09-10. All six codes have listed production in 2017–2019, but their exact dates and chassis ranges differ.
- **Markets:** Japan. Do not reuse N-BOX ranges, or extend this entry to GE/GR Fit or overseas Jazz.

## The issue

A deformed impeller can stop fuel delivery and stall the engine. The 2024-06-14 correction requires checking the pump identification mark and replacing applicable pumps: some vehicles in the published range already have countermeasure parts. Hybrid status or a transmission repair does not determine fuel-pump status.

## Symptoms an owner can detect

- Honda lists difficult starting, poor acceleration, vibration and stalling as reasons to contact a dealer.
- These symptoms alone do not identify the failed component. Do not keep driving to reproduce stalling; seek professional help.

## Severity

**Critical.** Loss of propulsion while driving is a safety concern.

## Frequency (honest assessment)

Neither the campaign population nor the vehicle's mileage establishes its failure probability. Do not describe all GK/GP Fits as defective.

## Fix and cost

- **Repair:** Ask Honda for applicability, pump-identification findings and the current remedy record. Fuel-system access and replacement are professional work.
- **Typical cost:** Confirm the individual recall remedy and terms with Honda. No verified retail price or promise that unrelated repairs are free is provided.

## Buyer checklist

- Request the **5423 fuel-pump identification result** and, where applicable, the replacement record.
- Distinguish "countermeasure part identified," "replacement completed," and "not checked." A chassis-range match alone proves none of these.
- Keep i-DCD clutch, transmission and software records separate from the fuel-pump remedy, especially on GP5/GP6 hybrids.
- Use the exact Fit chassis row and current dealer information; old parts-supply announcements do not establish today's appointment or completion date.
