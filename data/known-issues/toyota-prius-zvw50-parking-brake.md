# Toyota Prius ZVW50/ZVW51/ZVW55 — parking brake cable recall

**English** | [日本語](./toyota-prius-zvw50-parking-brake.ja.md)

## Scope

- **Vehicles:** Prius DAA-ZVW50, DAA-ZVW51 and DAA-ZVW55 in recall 3905.
- **Engines/components:** Parking brake cable retention at the operating lever.
- **Production window:** Listed chassis subranges within 2015-10-26 to 2016-10-03. These are production dates, not purchase or registration dates.
- **Markets:** Japan. This entry does not cover ZVW30, ZVW52 PHV, or later Prius generations.

## The issue

Vibration can detach the inadequately retained parking brake cable from its lever, leaving the parking brake inoperative. Toyota's remedy adds a retaining clip. This is separate from hydraulic brake actuator campaigns or other Prius recalls.

## Symptoms an owner can detect

- A reported failure of the parking brake to hold warrants professional inspection; a vehicle sitting still once does not prove the remedy was completed.
- Do not test for rollaway on a slope. If parking security is uncertain, obtain professional assistance rather than deliberately reproducing the fault.

## Severity

**Critical.** Loss of parking-brake operation creates a vehicle-movement risk.

## Frequency (honest assessment)

The listed production scope does not establish a failure rate for an individual car. Neither age nor quiet operation proves applicability or completion.

## Fix and cost

- **Repair:** Have Toyota verify recall 3905 applicability and the clip-installation record. Brake inspection and installation are professional work; no adjustment or fitting procedure is provided here.
- **Typical cost:** The recall describes free remedy work for applicable vehicles. Confirm current individual status; unrelated brake work needs its own estimate.

## Buyer checklist

- Ask specifically for the recall **3905 parking-cable remedy**, not just a generic "brake recall done" statement.
- Compare the exact production date and chassis row with Toyota's lookup; a listed range can include vehicles outside the recall.
- Separate the parking-cable record from actuator, software, or other campaign records.
- Do not treat a 2017 registration as proof of 2017 production, or missing coverage here as proof of no recalls.

A Japan-market Prius ZVW51 was first registered in 2017, but its production date is unknown. The seller says a brake recall was done. What evidence would establish whether parking-cable recall 3905 applies and was completed?
