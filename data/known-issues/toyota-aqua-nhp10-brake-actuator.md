# Toyota Aqua NHP10/NHP10H — brake actuator service campaign

**English** | [日本語](./toyota-aqua-nhp10-brake-actuator.ja.md)

## Scope

- **Vehicles:** Japan-market Aqua DAA/6AA-NHP10 and DAA/6AA-NHP10H in Toyota's 2025-06-20 service campaign.
- **Engines/components:** Electrically controlled brake actuator, booster and booster pump.
- **Production window:** NHP10 listed subranges span 2011-11-07 to 2021-06-04; NHP10H subranges span 2017-05-10 to 2021-05-31. Use the exact row, not these outer bounds, for individual checks.
- **Markets:** Japan. Do not apply these ranges to export-market Prius c or the later MXPK Aqua.

## The issue

Frequent brake operation can wear the actuator's hydraulic-control valves earlier than expected. Several warning lights may remain on; continued use can lead to a warning buzzer and reduced braking assistance. This is a **service campaign**, not a recall notice or automatic authorization to replace every listed vehicle's parts.

## Symptoms an owner can detect

- Persistent multiple brake-related warning lights, rather than the normal brief startup lamp check.
- Record existing warnings and contact Toyota promptly. A buzzer or changed braking response needs professional assistance; do not perform a road test to reproduce it.

## Severity

**Critical.** Braking assistance can be affected. This classification describes the potential consequence, not the probability of failure.

## Frequency (honest assessment)

The notice does not establish an individual failure probability. Listed vehicles are not a count of failed actuators.

## Fix and cost

- **Repair:** Toyota describes replacement of the booster assembly and pump, and a revised specified fluid, when the relevant warning-light condition occurs. Brake-system diagnosis and work belong to qualified technicians.
- **Typical cost:** The campaign describes no-charge work under its conditions. Confirm applicability and the required inspection with Toyota; no retail estimate or blanket free-repair promise is provided here.

## Buyer checklist

- Keep **service campaign** distinct from recall and warranty extension.
- An asterisk (*) in Toyota's completion-status field is not proof of repair completion; request the actual inspection and repair records.
- Ask whether the relevant warning lights have stayed on and what was diagnosed. No warning today is not a repair receipt.
- Confirm the exact type designation, production date and current campaign conditions privately with Toyota. Do not post a full chassis number in a public prompt.
