# Nissan Serena C26 — ECO motor bearing recall

**English** | [日本語](./nissan-serena-c26-eco-motor-bearing.ja.md)

## Scope

- **Vehicles:** ECO-motor idle-stop cars in the C26/FC26/NC26/FNC26 rows of recall 5268. The C26 family name alone is not enough to establish eligibility.
- **Engines/components:** ECO motor internal bearings and seals.
- **Production window:** C26: 2010-09-20 to 2012-06-30; FC26: 2010-11-15 to 2012-06-30; NC26: 2010-09-20 to 2016-06-16; FNC26: 2010-10-28 to 2016-06-17. Each requires chassis confirmation.
- **Markets:** Japan. Do not substitute these rows for HC26/HFC26, later Serenas, or the separate X-Trail rows.

## The issue

Bearing-seal failure can lead to noise and ECO motor failure, potentially stopping the engine. The notice also describes a fire risk when surrounding components carry combustible contamination. A temporary heat shield and final motor replacement are different stages of the remedy.

## Symptoms an owner can detect

- Engine-bay noise, a flashing idle-stop indicator, or a charging warning lamp are reasons to contact the Nissan workshop.
- Record existing warnings rather than restarting repeatedly or assuming that disabling idle-stop resolves the underlying defect.

## Severity

**Critical.** The notice includes engine stoppage and a conditional fire hazard.

## Frequency (honest assessment)

The cited manufacturer information does not establish a failure probability for this vehicle or a mileage-based failure rate. A campaign population is not a count of failed vehicles; do not describe every example as defective.

## Fix and cost

- **Repair:** Confirm both interim and final actions with Nissan. The announced final remedy is a revised ECO motor; parts scheduling can change. Do not turn a temporary heat-shield installation into an “all work completed” statement.
- **Typical cost:** Japan: confirm individual eligibility and the current remedy terms with the dealer first. No verified general retail price is supplied. Do not promise that all associated repairs are free; obtain an itemized quote for work outside the remedy.

## Buyer checklist

- Read the model code, not only the vehicle name, and obtain the current chassis-specific result.
- Ask separately whether the heat shield was fitted and whether the revised motor was installed.
- Confirm current parts and appointment availability with the dealer instead of relying on an old schedule.

Example prompt for an evidence-based check:

```text
2011 Japan-market Serena FC26: the record says a heat shield was installed for 5268. Prepare a purchase checklist that keeps interim protection and final ECO motor replacement separate.
```
